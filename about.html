About Our Digital Notice Board

Our Mission

The SCE Digital Notice Board is designed to be the central hub for all academic, cultural, and sports activities within Samtse College of Education. Our primary mission is to streamline event communication, making it easier for students, faculty, and staff to stay informed, register for events, and manage their schedules effectively.

Key Features

We provide a unified platform to manage the diverse range of events at SCE:

  • Centralized Event Listings: Filter and view events across three main categories: Academic, Sports & Games, and Cultural.
  • Effortless Registration: Quick sign-up for any event with instant confirmation.
  • Smart Notifications: Integration with user calendars (via ICS file download) and email systems to ensure no event is missed.
  • Admin Control: A dedicated panel for event organizers to post, manage, and communicate changes instantly.

Commitment to the Community

This platform is built by and for the SCE community. We are committed to fostering a vibrant campus life by improving accessibility and engagement in all college initiatives. For technical support or feedback, please use the contact details provided in the footer.
