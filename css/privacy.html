Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the use of the **SCE Digital Notice Board** event management system, provided by Samtse College of Education. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you register on the platform or express an interest in obtaining information about our events.

  • **Personal Data: Full Name, Email Address (for identification and communication).
  • **Registration Data: Records of the specific events you register for.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information collected is used solely for internal college event management purposes, including:

  • To facilitate user account creation and login.
  • To register you for specific events and manage attendance.
  • To send event notifications (e.g., event updates, time changes) via email and calendar downloads (ICS files).

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not share your personal data with any third parties outside of the Samtse College of Education administration and relevant event organizers. Your email is only used for communication directly related to college activities.

4. Security of Your Information

While this prototype uses browser Local Storage for demonstration purposes, a production version will employ secure server-side databases and encryption to safeguard your information against unauthorized access, use, or disclosure.

5. Contact Information

If you have questions or comments about this policy, please contact us at the college administration office.
