Privacy Statement

       

Welcome to the Samtse College Event Management System ("we", "our", or "the website") is developed to provide students and faculty with timely information about upcoming events in our college. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when using our system.

       

1. Information We Collect

       

Our website is designed for public access to event details. We do not require user login or registration. We collect only the following information:

       
               
  • Event Details: Title, date, time, venue, and description of events added by authorized admins/organizers.
    •            
  • Optional Email Addresses: If students or faculty choose to subscribe to email reminders or Google Calendar notifications.
    •        
       

We do not collect personal student or faculty data such as IDs, passwords, or academic records.

       

2. How We Use Information

       
               
  • To display upcoming college events on the website.
    •            
  • To send reminders or notifications if users opt-in via email or Google Calendar.
    •            
  • To allow authorized admins/organizers to add, update, or delete events.
    •            
  • To improve event communication and reduce reliance on physical notice boards.
    •        
       

3. Sharing & Disclosure

         
               
  • Event information is publicly visible to all students and faculty.
    •            
  • Email addresses, if collected for reminders, are used only for notification purposes.
    •            
  • We do not sell, rent, or share user data with third parties.
    •          
       

4. Security Measures

         
               
  • Only authorized admins/organizers can access the event management (Add/Edit/Delete) features.
    •            
  • Event data is stored securely to prevent unauthorized tampering.
    •            
  • Optional email data, if collected, is kept confidential and encrypted.
    •          
       

5. Cookies & Tracking

         
               
  • Our website currently does not use cookies or third-party tracking.
    •            
  • If cookies or analytics tools are added in the future, this policy will be updated.
    •          
       

6. Your Choices

         
               
  • Students and faculty may opt out of receiving email reminders or calendar notifications at any time.
    •            
  • Since the website does not use accounts, no personal profile data is stored.
    •          
       

7. Updates to This Policy

         

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect system improvements or policy changes. Updates will be posted here with a revised effective date.
